# Site Architecture

Status: Draft strategy, Phase 1.

## Hub-and-Spoke Model

Four intersecting systems: Service hubs, Persona hubs, Location hubs, Offer
landing pages. Combined pages are created only when they have distinct
search intent, meaningful original content, a legitimate audience need,
sufficient differentiation, a clear internal-linking role,
compliance-approved claims, and no doorway-page characteristics. No
automatic service×persona×location permutation generation.

## Main Brand Hub

Home, About, Our Approach, Who We Help, Services, Insights, Resources, FAQ,
Contact, Schedule a Conversation, Form CRS / required disclosures link,
Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Accessibility Statement, Regulatory/firm
disclosures, Business continuity/security information where required.

## Service Hubs (approved candidates, pending firm confirmation)

Financial Planning, Wealth Management, Retirement Planning, Investment
Strategy, Business-Owner Planning, Executive Financial Planning, Business
Succession Planning, Estate-Planning Coordination, Insurance and Risk
Planning, Education-Funding Planning, Charitable Planning, Tax-Aware
Financial Planning (never framed as tax preparation/accounting/legal
advice).

Each hub template includes: intent-aligned title, intro explanation, who it
may be appropriate for, common planning considerations, the firm's
approved process, related personas, related locations, educational
resources, FAQs, low-pressure CTA, relevant disclosures, structured-data
recommendation, internal links, and compliance annotations (draft-only).

## Persona Hubs

High-net-worth individuals/families, business owners, healthcare
professionals, physicians, dentists, medical-practice owners, manufacturing
executives, manufacturing-business owners, contractors/trade-business
owners, corporate executives, pre-retirees, retirees. Educational framing
only ("may encounter," never "need our proprietary strategy").

## Offer Landing Pages

Financial Review, Retirement Review, Business-Owner Financial Review,
Business Fee Audit (kept technically/legally separate; requires its own
entity-disclosure approval before build beyond scaffold).

## Combined High-Intent Pages — Candidates Requiring Validation

See `KEYWORD-AND-INTENT-MAP.csv` for the build/defer/merge/reject
recommendation on each candidate (e.g., "Financial Planning for Physicians
in Cleveland," "Wealth Management for Business Owners in Greater
Cleveland," "Retirement Planning for Executives in Shaker Heights").

## Page-Creation Checklist (applies to every combined page)

1. Distinct search intent confirmed
2. Meaningful, original content plan
3. Legitimate audience need articulated
4. Differentiation from adjacent pages confirmed
5. Internal-linking role defined
6. Compliance-approved claims only
7. No doorway-page characteristics
8. Recommendation recorded: build / defer / merge / reject
