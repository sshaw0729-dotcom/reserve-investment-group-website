## 1. PURPOSE

Reserve Investment Group, Inc. (“Reserve,” “RIG,” or the “Company”) maintains this Zero-Compensation Policy for Unaffiliated Securities and Investment Referrals to establish clear standards governing referrals, introductions, and similar activities involving persons or organizations that are not affiliated with Reserve and are not acting as appropriately registered securities or investment professionals on Reserve’s behalf.

The purpose of this Policy is to:

1. prevent direct or indirect payment of compensation for securities or investment referrals by Reserve;
2. prevent unregistered or unauthorized persons from engaging in securities solicitation, investment-advisory solicitation, investment recommendations, or other regulated activities on Reserve’s behalf;
3. establish clear boundaries between a permissible introduction and regulated securities or investment activity;
4. prevent referral relationships from being presented in a manner that could create a misleading impression of affiliation, endorsement, agency, partnership, or regulatory approval;
5. establish appropriate controls for testimonials, endorsements, advertising, social media, and other marketing involving referral sources;
6. protect prospective clients, clients, Reserve, its personnel, and any affiliated or associated regulated financial-services entities; and
7. maintain appropriate documentation and supervisory controls.

Reserve has voluntarily adopted a zero-compensation standard for the unaffiliated securities and investment referrals covered by this Policy.

## 2. POLICY STATEMENT

Reserve Investment Group, Inc. will not directly or indirectly provide compensation or anything of value to an unaffiliated person or organization in exchange for:

- referring a prospective investment client;
- introducing a prospective investment client;
- soliciting a prospective investment client;
- generating investment-related leads;
- recommending Reserve or an investment professional associated with Reserve;
- recommending securities or investment-advisory services;
- causing or encouraging a person to open an investment or securities account;
- causing or encouraging a person to enter into an investment-advisory relationship; or
- completing, facilitating, or influencing a securities or investment transaction.

This prohibition applies regardless of whether a referral ultimately results in a new client, account, advisory relationship, investment, securities transaction, insurance transaction, or revenue to Reserve or any associated financial professional.

No employee, officer, director, representative, contractor, or other person acting on behalf of Reserve may circumvent this Policy through an indirect payment, reciprocal arrangement, third party, affiliated business, reimbursement, marketing arrangement, gift, charitable contribution, business opportunity, or other transfer of economic value.

## 3. DEFINITION OF AN UNAFFILIATED REFERRAL SOURCE

For purposes of this Policy, an “Unaffiliated Referral Source” means an individual or organization that:

1. is not an employee of Reserve;
2. is not acting as an authorized representative or agent of Reserve;
3. is not under common ownership or control with Reserve solely by virtue of the referral relationship;
4. has no authority to bind Reserve;
5. receives no compensation from Reserve for making the referral; and
6. is limited to making an introduction in accordance with this Policy.

Examples may include, subject to applicable law and this Policy:

- attorneys;
- certified public accountants;
- tax professionals;
- real estate professionals;
- mortgage professionals;
- bankers;
- business owners;
- consultants;
- community organizations;
- professional associations;
- existing business contacts;
- clients or customers;
- friends or acquaintances; and
- other independent professionals or organizations.

The inclusion of a person within one of these categories does not automatically make a referral permissible.

## 4. ZERO-COMPENSATION REQUIREMENT

Reserve shall not provide an Unaffiliated Referral Source with any payment, compensation, economic benefit, or thing of value in consideration for securities or investment referrals.

Prohibited compensation includes, but is not limited to:

- cash; checks; commissions; referral fees; finder’s fees; solicitor fees; success fees;
- transaction-based compensation; revenue sharing; asset-based compensation;
- percentages of advisory fees; percentages of securities commissions; bonuses based upon referrals;
- gift cards; prepaid cards; prizes; awards having economic value;
- free or discounted services; rebates; credits;
- expense reimbursements connected to referrals; entertainment provided in consideration for referrals; paid travel;
- marketing allowances; advertising subsidies;
- lead-purchase arrangements designed to compensate the source for particular referrals;
- reduced fees provided because referrals were made;
- charitable contributions made at the direction of a referral source in consideration for referrals;
- payments to a business, relative, employee, affiliate, charity, or other third party at the direction of the referral source;
- reciprocal payments; business opportunities provided as consideration for referrals; or
- any other direct or indirect economic benefit conditioned upon, connected with, or provided in consideration for a securities or investment referral.

The fact that a payment is described as a “marketing fee,” “consulting fee,” “advertising fee,” “business-development fee,” “administrative fee,” “thank-you payment,” or similar term does not make the payment permissible if it is actually connected to referrals.

**Substance controls over terminology.**

## 5. RECIPROCAL REFERRALS

Reserve recognizes that independent professionals may naturally refer individuals to one another in the ordinary course of business.

However, Reserve shall not enter into an agreement, understanding, quota, expectation, or other arrangement under which:

> “You send clients to us and we will send clients to you.”

Reserve personnel may independently introduce a client or prospect to another professional when they reasonably believe the introduction may be helpful and when otherwise permitted by applicable law and firm policies.

Such an introduction must not be consideration or compensation for referrals received by Reserve.

There shall be:

- no referral quotas;
- no required number of reciprocal referrals;
- no dollar-for-dollar exchange;
- no tracking intended to equalize economic value;
- no obligation to return referrals;
- no preferential treatment based upon referral volume; and
- no promise that Reserve will reciprocate.

## 6. PERMITTED REFERRAL ACTIVITY

Unless the individual is separately authorized and appropriately registered or licensed to perform additional activities, an Unaffiliated Referral Source should generally limit the referral to a simple introduction.

A permitted introduction may include:

- providing Reserve’s publicly available contact information;
- providing an approved Reserve business card;
- directing a person to Reserve’s public website;
- introducing the prospective client to an authorized Reserve representative;
- forwarding an approved Reserve informational communication without alteration, where permitted;
- stating that the referral source knows of Reserve; or
- suggesting that the person contact Reserve to determine independently whether Reserve may be able to assist.

A simple introduction might state:

> “Reserve Investment Group may be a resource worth speaking with. I can give you their contact information if you would like.”

The referral source should then allow appropriately authorized financial professionals to handle all substantive securities or investment discussions.

## 7. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES BY UNAFFILIATED REFERRAL SOURCES

Unless separately authorized and appropriately registered or licensed to conduct the activity, an Unaffiliated Referral Source may not act on Reserve’s behalf to:

**A. Recommend securities**

The referral source may not recommend: stocks; bonds; mutual funds; exchange-traded funds; variable products; alternative investments; private securities; investment strategies; managed accounts; securities portfolios; or any other security or investment product.

**B. Provide investment advice**

The referral source may not advise a prospect concerning: what investments to buy, sell, or hold; asset allocation; portfolio construction; investment risk; investment suitability; investment objectives; retirement investment strategies; expected investment performance; or whether a particular investment strategy is appropriate.

**C. Solicit securities business**

The referral source may not: urge a prospect to purchase a security; persuade a prospect to open an investment account; recommend that a prospect hire a particular investment adviser; negotiate terms; participate in the securities transaction; complete securities forms for the prospect; collect securities orders; transmit securities orders; discuss transaction pricing; close an investment sale; participate in account-opening decisions; or otherwise perform activities requiring securities registration.

**D. Make investment-performance representations**

The referral source may not make statements regarding: expected investment returns; historical performance unless contained in specifically approved materials; guaranteed performance; investment safety; likelihood of profit; likelihood of avoiding loss; superior investment performance; market predictions; or expected financial outcomes.

**E. Make unauthorized statements about Reserve**

An Unaffiliated Referral Source may not represent that: Reserve guarantees results; Reserve is endorsed by a government agency; the SEC, FINRA, any state regulator, or another regulator has approved Reserve; Reserve is “risk free”; Reserve is “the best” adviser or investment firm; Reserve will outperform other advisers; any particular investment outcome is assured; or the referral source is authorized to speak on behalf of Reserve.

## 8. NO AGENCY OR REPRESENTATIVE AUTHORITY

An Unaffiliated Referral Source is not, solely because the individual makes a referral: an employee of Reserve; an agent of Reserve; a securities representative of Reserve; an investment adviser representative of Reserve; a solicitor acting on Reserve’s behalf; an authorized representative of Reserve; a partner of Reserve; a joint venturer with Reserve; or authorized to enter into agreements on behalf of Reserve.

No Unaffiliated Referral Source has authority to bind Reserve or make commitments, promises, representations, or warranties on Reserve’s behalf.

## 9. USE OF “PARTNER” AND SIMILAR TERMINOLOGY

Reserve personnel should not describe an unaffiliated referral source using terminology that could reasonably create a misleading impression regarding the relationship.

Absent specific Compliance approval, avoid descriptions such as: “partner”; “strategic partner”; “preferred partner”; “approved partner”; “financial partner”; “investment partner”; “authorized representative”; “agent”; “affiliate”; “preferred adviser”; “approved adviser”; or similar terminology suggesting formal affiliation, endorsement, supervision, or agency.

Preferred terminology includes: “Independent Third-Party Professional”; “Independent Third-Party Resource”; “Unaffiliated Professional”; “Unaffiliated Referral Source”; or “Independent Professional.”

## 10. TESTIMONIALS, ENDORSEMENTS, AND MARKETING

The fact that an Unaffiliated Referral Source receives no compensation does not automatically permit Reserve to use that person’s statements in advertising or marketing.

Any testimonial, endorsement, recommendation, referral statement, quotation, video, social-media post, review, or similar communication that Reserve proposes to publish, repost, share, feature, promote, incorporate into advertising, place on Reserve’s website, use on a landing page, include in an email campaign, use in paid advertising, or otherwise disseminate as part of Reserve’s marketing must receive Compliance review before use.

Reserve shall evaluate such communications under all applicable advertising and marketing requirements, including rules governing testimonials and endorsements.

No employee may assume that an uncompensated testimonial or endorsement is automatically exempt from regulatory requirements.

## 11. SOCIAL MEDIA

Unaffiliated Referral Sources are not authorized to conduct social-media marketing on Reserve’s behalf unless specifically approved under a separate written arrangement and applicable compliance procedures.

Referral sources should not be instructed to: post promotional investment claims; make investment recommendations; publish investment-performance claims; create testimonials on demand; post pre-written endorsements that have not received required review; respond to investment questions on Reserve’s behalf; or represent themselves as part of Reserve.

Reserve personnel must not provide referral sources with prohibited scripts or talking points designed to accomplish indirectly what Reserve or its regulated personnel could not permissibly do directly.

## 12. CLIENT REVIEWS AND ORGANIC COMMENTS

Reserve will not compensate a client, prospect, professional, or other person for providing a favorable online review, social-media comment, endorsement, rating, or testimonial concerning Reserve’s investment-related services under this Policy.

Reserve personnel must not: condition any benefit upon a positive review; offer compensation for a favorable rating; tell a referral source what substantive investment claim to make; selectively alter a review to make it misleading; or present an uncompensated review in Reserve advertising without conducting the required Compliance analysis.

Organic public comments must be handled in accordance with Reserve’s separate advertising, social-media, and communications policies.

## 13. REFERRAL SOURCE DISCLOSURE

Where Reserve identifies or lists an independent third-party professional on its website, marketing material, or other public communication, the following or substantially similar disclosure should be used when appropriate:

**Independent Third-Party Disclosure**

> “This individual or organization is independent of and unaffiliated with Reserve Investment Group, Inc. Reserve Investment Group, Inc. does not pay or receive referral fees, commissions, or other compensation in connection with the unpaid referral relationship described here. You are under no obligation to use any referred professional or service provider and should independently evaluate any provider before engaging their services.”

Compliance may modify the disclosure depending upon the relationship and applicable regulatory requirements.

## 14. NO GUARANTEE OR ENDORSEMENT OF THIRD PARTIES

When Reserve introduces a client or prospect to an independent third party, the introduction does not constitute a guarantee regarding that third party’s: qualifications; licensing; competence; advice; services; products; pricing; performance; conduct; or results.

Reserve personnel should exercise reasonable care when making professional introductions but should not represent that Reserve guarantees the work or conduct of an independent third party.

Clients and prospects remain responsible for independently evaluating professionals they choose to engage.

## 15. NO CLIENT OBLIGATION

No client or prospect is required to use a professional introduced by Reserve.

A person’s decision to accept a referral, decline a referral, use another professional, or use no outside professional shall not affect the person’s access to services otherwise available from Reserve, subject to ordinary legal and business requirements.

## 16. REFERRAL INFORMATION AND CLIENT PRIVACY

Reserve personnel shall not provide confidential or nonpublic client information to a referral source without appropriate authorization and compliance with applicable privacy requirements.

A referral relationship does not give an unaffiliated person access to: account information; portfolio information; Social Security numbers; financial plans; income information; net worth; investment holdings; tax documents; client communications; or other nonpublic personal information.

Whenever practical, Reserve should allow the client or prospect to initiate contact with the third party rather than transmitting confidential information unnecessarily.

## 17. PROHIBITED INDIRECT COMPENSATION

Reserve will evaluate the substance of relationships rather than merely their formal description.

The following may constitute prohibited indirect compensation when provided because of referrals and therefore require Compliance review: sponsorship payments; shared advertising expenses; excessive meals or entertainment; consulting payments; event sponsorships; speaking fees; free office space; free services; discounted services; preferred-vendor arrangements; cross-marketing subsidies; payments to family members; payments to affiliated companies; charitable contributions directed by a referral source; special business opportunities; or other arrangements providing measurable economic benefit.

No arrangement may be structured for the purpose of avoiding this Policy.

## 18. ORDINARY BUSINESS COURTESIES

Nothing in this Policy is intended to prohibit ordinary professional interaction permitted under applicable laws and applicable firm policies.

However, gifts, meals, entertainment, events, sponsorships, or other business courtesies must never be conditioned on: the number of referrals made; referral volume; assets obtained; accounts opened; advisory agreements executed; securities transactions completed; revenue generated; or investment business received.

When there is uncertainty about whether an item could reasonably be viewed as compensation for referrals, it must be submitted to Compliance before it is provided.

## 19. REFERRAL LOG

Reserve should maintain a reasonable record of recurring professional referral relationships.

The record may include: name of referral source; business or organization; profession; contact information; date relationship was established; confirmation that compensation is not paid; known professional licensing information, where relevant; Compliance review date; marketing or website use, if any; identified conflicts of interest; and termination date, if applicable.

Reserve is not required by this Policy to treat every isolated unsolicited introduction by a member of the public as a formal referral relationship. Compliance may determine the appropriate level of documentation based upon the nature and frequency of activity.

## 20. PERIODIC CERTIFICATION

At least annually, or at another interval established by Compliance, relevant Reserve personnel should certify that they have not: paid prohibited referral compensation; promised prohibited referral compensation; entered into undisclosed reciprocal referral arrangements; permitted unauthorized persons to solicit securities business; knowingly allowed a referral source to hold itself out improperly; used unapproved testimonials or endorsements; or created indirect compensation arrangements intended to circumvent this Policy.

Any exception or potential violation must be disclosed promptly.

## 21. COMPLIANCE REVIEW

The following arrangements require Compliance review before implementation: any written referral agreement; any recurring referral program; any arrangement involving securities or investment leads; any proposed payment involving a referral source; any shared marketing arrangement; any joint event involving a referral source; any reciprocal business-development arrangement; any website listing of outside professionals; any testimonial or endorsement; any referral-related social-media campaign; any arrangement involving a government entity or government plan; any referral involving private securities or capital raising; any arrangement in which the referral source participates in substantive investment discussions; and any arrangement that could reasonably create uncertainty regarding whether compensation or regulated activity is involved.

When in doubt, personnel must seek Compliance review before, rather than after, engaging in the activity.

## 22. REGISTRATION AND LICENSING

Nothing in this Policy authorizes any person to engage in conduct requiring: broker-dealer registration; registered representative status; investment-adviser registration; investment-adviser-representative registration; insurance licensing; municipal-advisor registration; commodities registration; or any other professional or regulatory license.

A zero-compensation arrangement does not by itself make otherwise regulated conduct permissible.

If a person’s activities exceed a simple introduction, Reserve must evaluate the arrangement before permitting the activity to continue.

## 23. GOVERNMENT AND PUBLIC-ENTITY BUSINESS

Referrals involving government entities, public pension plans, government investment accounts, elected officials, candidates, or persons connected with the selection of investment advisers present additional regulatory considerations.

No Reserve employee or referral source may establish or participate in a government-related referral arrangement without prior Compliance approval.

This Policy does not supersede any applicable political-contribution, pay-to-play, municipal-securities, lobbying, ethics, or government-contracting requirements.

## 24. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER POLICIES AND REGULATED AFFILIATIONS

This Policy governs Reserve Investment Group, Inc.’s internal approach to unpaid, unaffiliated securities and investment referrals.

It does not supersede: federal securities laws; SEC rules; FINRA rules; state securities laws; state investment-adviser requirements; state insurance laws; broker-dealer policies; investment-adviser policies; employment agreements; outside-business-activity requirements; private-securities-transaction requirements; communications policies; advertising policies; social-media policies; or requirements imposed by any financial institution with which a Reserve employee, officer, owner, contractor, or representative is associated.

Where another applicable law, regulation, firm policy, supervisory requirement, or contractual obligation is more restrictive than this Policy, the more restrictive requirement controls.

## 25. NO WORKAROUNDS

No Reserve employee or representative may accomplish indirectly what this Policy prohibits directly.

Examples include: paying another company owned by the referral source; paying the referral source’s spouse or family member; inflating payment for unrelated services; purchasing unnecessary services as consideration for referrals; routing compensation through another business; exchanging referrals pursuant to an undisclosed quota; directing charitable donations in exchange for introductions; or disguising referral compensation as marketing, consulting, sponsorship, or administrative expenses.

Potential circumvention must be reported immediately to Compliance.

## 26. EXCEPTIONS

There are no automatic exceptions to the zero-compensation requirement.

Any proposed exception must: (1) be submitted in writing; (2) describe the proposed relationship and all direct and indirect economic benefits; (3) identify the activities the referral source will perform; (4) be reviewed under applicable securities and investment-adviser laws; (5) receive all required regulatory, broker-dealer, investment-adviser, employer, or supervisory approvals; and (6) receive written approval from Reserve’s designated Compliance authority before implementation.

Absent written approval, the zero-compensation requirement remains in effect.

## 27. REPORTING POTENTIAL VIOLATIONS

Reserve personnel must promptly report any known or suspected violation of this Policy, including: unauthorized payments; promises of payment; unauthorized solicitation; undisclosed reciprocal arrangements; misleading representations by referral sources; unauthorized testimonials or endorsements; unauthorized investment recommendations; improper use of Reserve’s name or branding; or attempts to circumvent the Policy.

Good-faith reporting is expected as part of Reserve’s compliance culture.

## 28. REMEDIATION

Upon discovering a potential violation, Reserve may take appropriate remedial steps, which may include: immediately stopping the referral activity; terminating the relationship; removing marketing material; correcting public statements; documenting the issue; notifying applicable supervisory personnel; determining whether client communication is appropriate; consulting securities counsel; notifying an applicable broker-dealer, investment adviser, insurer, regulator, or other entity where required; and implementing additional controls, training, or supervision.

## 29. TRAINING

Reserve personnel involved in business development, marketing, professional networking, client introductions, or referral relationships should receive periodic instruction concerning this Policy.

Training should emphasize the difference between a permissible introduction and solicitation, investment advice, securities recommendations, transaction participation, or compensated referral activity.

## 30. POLICY PRINCIPLE

Reserve’s standard is straightforward:

> Introductions may be relationship-driven. They may not be compensation-driven.

An unaffiliated person may introduce someone to Reserve when legally permissible, but Reserve will not pay that person for securities or investment referrals under this Policy.

The individual making the introduction should remain outside the substantive investment-advice, recommendation, account-opening, and securities-transaction process unless independently authorized, appropriately registered or licensed, and approved to perform those activities.

## 31. REQUIRED REFERRAL-SOURCE LANGUAGE

Where Reserve provides written guidance to an Unaffiliated Referral Source, Reserve may use the following:

**Permitted Introduction**

> “You are welcome to introduce someone to Reserve Investment Group, Inc. if you believe a conversation may be helpful. Reserve does not pay you for the introduction. Please do not recommend investments, discuss specific securities or investment strategies, provide investment advice, promise investment results, or represent that you work for or act on behalf of Reserve. The individual should speak directly with an appropriately authorized financial professional regarding any investment-related questions.”
