## Current release rule

The compliance manifest is the authoritative release gate. A page may only receive a status beginning with `Production approved` when its actual required approval has been recorded. Technical fixes, attorney-reviewed draft language, or a general standing copy approval are not substitutes for a page-level approval record where `HUMAN-APPROVAL-MATRIX.md` requires one.
